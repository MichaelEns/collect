Joe's Collection privacy policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

The skill stores collection owner or sharing codes supplied by the user, associated only with Amazon's skill-specific user identifier. Amazon does not receive those codes in the skill package.

A code is sent to the collection service only to retrieve or, when its role allows, update the selected checklist. On Echo Show, the skill may display privately stored toy photographs through encrypted links that expire after fifteen minutes. The widget stores only the selected collection name and aggregate found/total count in Alexa's on-device Data Store. The skill does not receive or store voice recordings, names, email addresses, or Amazon account credentials, and it does not sell or share data for advertising.

Saying "forget my sharing code" deletes the selected saved code. Saying "forget every collection" deletes all saved codes for that Alexa user. Collection records remain managed by the collection tracker.
